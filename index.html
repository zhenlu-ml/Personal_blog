Zhen Lu

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · University of Massachusetts Boston

I work on machine learning for structured and sequential data: Transformers, graph neural networks, and self-supervised pretraining for time series. My first-author work at IEEE BigData 2025 infers latent orderings (pseudotime) from tabular biological data with a causal-mask Transformer.

Lately I've also been building LLM agents that call tools, check their own work, and hand the final decision back to a person.

Roameo: a multi-agent LLM travel planner

Agentic AI Hackathon, team project · 2026
  • Seven agents (intake, research, composer, critic, feedback, guide, orchestrator). The LLM handles language and tool choice, and budget math stays deterministic.
  • Bounded tool-calling loop over flight, hotel, event, attraction and weather tools, a critic-driven repair loop, and human approval before anything is exported.
  • Scenario-based evaluation harness: 23/23 core scenarios pass in LLM mode.

Self-supervised pretraining for remaining-useful-life prediction In progress

First-author research · UMass Boston

Pretraining for predictive maintenance on NASA C-MAPSS that needs no failure labels: a Bradley–Terry–Luce soft ranking loss over within-engine temporal order, plus condition-aware prototype alignment. Benchmarked against 13 supervised, self-supervised and foundation-model baselines.
